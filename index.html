About the platform

What is Mika AI?

Mika AI is a powerful, all-in-one artificial intelligence suite built for Windows. Unlike tools that depend on expensive subscriptions and constant cloud usage, Mika AI runs locally on your own computer — your data never has to leave your device.

It combines Llama 3 for conversation, reasoning and code with Stable Diffusion for image and video generation, delivering a premium creative workflow at zero cost. Whether you are a student, creator or researcher, Mika AI is built to empower you.

Local execution is the foundation, not the ceiling. Mika AI is evolving into a hybrid AI platform: alongside the on-device Llama 3 and Stable Diffusion engines, we are integrating Cloud API providers — beginning with the Anthropic Claude API — to deliver advanced reasoning, long-context document analysis and agentic workflows that exceed consumer hardware limits. Users choose the right engine per task: stay fully offline for privacy and zero cost, or enable cloud-backed intelligence on demand.

Before you install

System Requirements

Minimum Requirements

  • Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit)
  • Intel Core i5 (8th Gen) / AMD Ryzen 5
  • 8 GB RAM
  • 10 GB free space (SSD)

Recommended (Best Performance)

  • RAM: 16 GB or higher
  • GPU: NVIDIA graphics card (4 GB VRAM)
  • Internet connection for optional Cloud API features
  • * A GPU is optional, but recommended for faster video generation.

Privacy Policy

Your Privacy is Our Priority

Mika AI processes 99% of tasks offline using your computer's hardware. We do not collect, store or sell your chat history, generated images or personal files. All generated content is saved locally in your generated_outputs folder.

What We Store

Nothing personal. Your prompts, chats and media stay on your device. We retain only anonymous, aggregated usage statistics to diagnose crashes and improve stability.

Optional Cloud Features

Cloud API features (such as the Anthropic Claude API) are strictly opt-in. Nothing is transmitted off your PC unless you enable them, and they can be disabled at any time.

Your Rights

Delete your local data at any time by removing the Mika AI folder. For any privacy request, email admin@mika-ai.xyz.
